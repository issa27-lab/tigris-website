Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Tigris Auto Glass LLC (“we”, “us”), located at 1054 El Camino Ave, Sacramento, CA 95815, operates tigrisautoglass.com and our customer estimate, scheduling and payment portal.

Information we collect

When you request a quote or service, we collect your name, phone number, vehicle information, and details about the work requested. If you pay through our portal, payments are processed by our payment provider; we do not store card numbers.

How we use it

To prepare your estimate, schedule and perform the work, send you service-related text messages and calls, process payment, and keep records required by law or by insurers.

SMS / text messaging

If you provide your mobile number when requesting a quote or service, we may send you transactional text messages about your estimate, appointment, job status, and payment. Message frequency varies by job. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time or HELP for help. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We share information only with service providers who help us operate (for example, our messaging and payment processors) and as required by law.
