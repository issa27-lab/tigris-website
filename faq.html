Insurance & Pricing

Most California comprehensive auto policies cover windshield replacement and chip repair at $0 out-of-pocket. We bill all major insurers directly — State Farm, GEICO, Progressive, Allstate, Farmers, USAA, AAA, Mercury, and more. We can verify your coverage before booking.
In California, comprehensive glass claims do not typically affect your premium since they're not at-fault. That said, we recommend confirming with your insurance agent to be sure. We can help you understand your policy before you file.
No problem — we offer fair, transparent cash pricing for all our services. Call us with your vehicle year, make, and model, and we'll give you an honest quote in under 60 seconds.
Cost depends on your vehicle and whether ADAS calibration is required. Older vehicles without ADAS are typically less; newer vehicles with cameras and sensors are more. With insurance, most customers pay $0. Call for a real quote — we don't believe in hidden fees.

Technical & Warranty

Most 2018+ vehicles with lane-keeping, adaptive cruise, or auto-braking require ADAS calibration after windshield replacement. We're Autel-certified and can handle both static and dynamic calibration in-house — no second trip to the dealer required.
Most chips smaller than a quarter and not in the driver's direct line of sight can be repaired. Cracks longer than 6 inches, multiple chips, or damage in critical visibility areas usually require full replacement. Send us a photo for a quick assessment.
Every installation comes with a lifetime workmanship warranty. If anything we installed leaks, whistles, or fails for as long as you own the vehicle, we make it right at no charge. That includes seal failures, water intrusion, and wind noise.
We use fast-cure urethane that typically allows safe driving in 30–60 minutes after install. Your tech will tell you the exact safe-drive-away time at your appointment, based on temperature and humidity that day.
We use OEM-fit glass that meets or exceeds factory specifications. For insurance claims, we work with what your insurance approves — which is almost always OEM-equivalent quality. Have a specific OEM requirement? Just let us know upfront.
