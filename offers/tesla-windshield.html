How it works

Three steps to a new windshield.

STEP 1

Call and tell us your model year

We confirm your Model 3 or Model Y qualifies for the $630 price and reserve a slot — usually same day or next day.

STEP 2

Bring the car to El Camino Ave

Why Tesla owners come to Tigris

Tesla windshields are big, expensive at the dealer, and often booked out for weeks. We stock DOT-certified glass for the Model 3 and Model Y, so you’re not waiting on a parts order, and our technicians replace them regularly enough that the job is routine. That’s how we can offer one flat price and still back it with a lifetime workmanship warranty.

Why in shop only?

The $630 price is only possible because the work happens in our bay, where the glass is already on the shelf and the car is out of the sun and wind while the urethane cures. If you can’t make it in, we still offer mobile service for Teslas at our regular mobile pricing.

Newer Model 3 or Model Y?

2022 and newer cars use different glass, so the $630 price doesn’t apply, but we have special pricing for them too. Call with your year and we’ll quote it on the spot.

Insurance or the offer?

We bill most insurers directly with a $0 deductible on many comprehensive glass plans. This offer can’t be combined with a claim, so if your deductible is lower than $630, use your insurance; if it’s higher, or you’d rather not file, the offer is usually the better deal. Not sure? Ask us when you call and we’ll help you compare.

*$0 deductible depends on your policy’s comprehensive glass coverage. We verify coverage with your insurer before work begins.
